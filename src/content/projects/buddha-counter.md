## Background

The app was originally designed and published on Android by its founding developer as a simple, focused counter for Buddhist chanting and prostrations. To keep it maintained, I took it over, rewrote it in Flutter, and released an iPhone version with a redesigned interface.

## Features

- **Two interfaces, switch any time**
  - *Classic mode* keeps the original Android layout so long-time users feel at home.
  - *Modern mode* has manual count, auto count, dedication and a practice calendar as swipeable tabs, with a persistent chant player.
- **Manual counting** — one tap per recitation, with optional wooden-fish or bell sounds.
- **Auto counting** — counts along while a chant is playing.
- **Practice calendar** — every day's count is recorded.
- **Cross-platform** — iOS, Android, and an installable web app (PWA).
